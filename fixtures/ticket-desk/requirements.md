# Ticket Desk: supplied requirements

- T1. A visitor can open the booking form for an event from the events list.
- T2. A booking must be for between 1 and 6 tickets. Any other number must be rejected with a validation message, and no booking is created.
- T3. The email must contain an "@". Otherwise the booking is rejected with a validation message.
- T4. After a successful booking, a confirmation page shows the event, the name and the number of tickets.
- T5. A booking can be cancelled from its confirmation page after confirming. A cancelled booking no longer appears under My bookings.
