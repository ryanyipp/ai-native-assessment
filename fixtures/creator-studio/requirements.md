# Creator Studio: supplied requirements

These are the behaviours a reviewer may be given. They describe expectations,
not implementation details.

- R1. A creator can start a new draft from the dashboard.
- R2. A draft has a title and a caption.
- R3. Saving a draft keeps its title and caption. After a page reload, the saved
  values are still shown.
- R4. A creator can schedule a post from the dashboard by giving a post title and
  a publish date.
- R5. A publish date in the past must be rejected: the form shows a validation
  message and no post is scheduled. "Past" means before the studio date shown
  on the scheduling form.
